# Brand customisation

AURÉVAN, MONSOON, NOIR, BLOOM and SANTAL are creative working names. Check trademark and domain availability before use.

Edit the `products` array near the top of `app/page.tsx` to change product names, scent families, notes, descriptions and INR prices. Replace the CSS-drawn bottle artwork with original product photography once your actual products are manufactured. Update SEO in `app/layout.tsx`.

Confirm final fragrance formulas, product claims, volume, MRP, tax treatment, packaging labels and compliance with your manufacturer and professional advisers before launch.
